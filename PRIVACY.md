# MoveAlert Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

MoveAlert, previously named LocationLock, is developed by Ryan Forsyth. This policy explains how information is handled by the iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch apps, and when you contact us for support.

## Our approach

MoveAlert does not require an account, track your location, show advertising, or use third-party analytics. Core alarm monitoring takes place on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your motion readings, alarm photos, or custom spoken-message text, and we do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Information used on your device

- **Motion and charging:** MoveAlert uses device motion and charging status to detect the events you choose to monitor. It does not record a history of your movements or use location services.
- **Settings and alarm state:** preferences, your custom message text, purchase-unlock status, and limited app-use counters are stored locally. The counters support features such as in-app rating prompts; they are not sent to an analytics service. Alarm state is shared locally with the app's controls, and the Watch keeps cached alarm state and recent handled-alarm identifiers to avoid duplicate alerts.
- **Spoken messages:** custom messages are entered as text and spoken using the device's speech synthesis. MoveAlert does not record your microphone.
- **Authentication:** optional Face ID or Touch ID checks are handled by Apple's authentication system. MoveAlert receives the authentication result, not your biometric data.

## Optional camera and Photos features

MoveAlert takes a single photo when an alarm triggers only if you enable **Capture Photo** and grant camera permission. It does not record video or maintain a permanent in-app photo gallery. Captured images are held for alarm display and, if enabled, transfer to your Watch.

If you enable **Save to Photos** and allow the app to add photos, the image is saved to your device's Photos library. MoveAlert does not need access to browse your existing photo library. Saved images remain in Photos until you delete them and follow your normal Photos and iCloud settings, including any syncing or backup you have enabled.

If you enable **Send to Apple Watch**, the captured image is transferred to your paired Watch for display with the alarm. MoveAlert uses Apple's device-connectivity services for this transfer; images may be queued locally for delivery. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

## Apple Watch and notifications

The iPhone and its paired Watch exchange alarm status, commands, alarm reasons, and, when enabled, photos so the companion app can display alerts and control the alarm. Local notifications may show alarm information on your devices according to your notification settings. We do not run a remote notification server for these alerts.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. MoveAlert uses Apple's purchase verification to determine whether Pro is unlocked and stores that status locally. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple's handling of purchase and account information is described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Diagnostics and support

MoveAlert writes device-local diagnostic logs for troubleshooting. It does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. Depending on your Apple privacy and diagnostic-sharing settings, Apple may make crash reports, performance reports, and app-usage statistics available to the developer. These are used to investigate problems and improve the app. Apple's collection and sharing are governed by its privacy policy and your device settings. See [Apple's explanation of diagnostic sharing](https://support.apple.com/108971).

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and troubleshoot reported issues. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only as needed to resolve requests, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence by emailing the address below.

GitHub discussions are public. Information you post there, including your GitHub profile and message contents, is visible to others and handled under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). External websites and services linked from the app have their own privacy practices.

## Your choices and deleting data

- Turn off optional camera, photo-saving, Watch-photo, and alarm features in MoveAlert Settings.
- Revoke permissions available for MoveAlert in your device's Settings, and control notification previews there.
- Edit your custom message and preferences in the app. Local preferences and cached state remain until changed, replaced, or the relevant app data is removed. To remove app data, delete the app rather than offloading it; remove the companion app from your Watch as well if you use it. Device backups may retain or restore app data according to your Apple backup settings.
- Delete any saved alarm images in Photos, including **Recently Deleted** if you want to remove them there too. Uninstalling MoveAlert does not delete images already saved to Photos.
- Contact us to request deletion of support information you sent. We cannot access or remotely delete the alarm data stored on your devices. Apple purchase records and information you post to GitHub are managed through those services.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will update the date above and provide notice in the app or its release notes before material changes to our privacy practices take effect.
